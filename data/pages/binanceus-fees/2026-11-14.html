What are maker and taker fees?

Maker and taker fees are the two rates that set the cost of your trade on the exchange, based on how your order meets the order book.

  • A maker order adds liquidity: it rests on the order book until it fills, so you pay the lower maker fee.
  • A taker order removes liquidity: it fills immediately against an existing order, so you pay the taker fee.

Limit orders that stay on the order book are usually makers, while market orders are usually takers.

On the Tier 0 pair (only BNB/USD at this time), Binance.US charges a 0% maker fee and a 0.01% taker fee, no matter how much you trade. On all other pairs, the rate is 0% maker and 0.02% taker, which lowers to 0.01% taker once your 30-day trading volume hits $500 million or more.

For additional details, see our guide on maker and taker fees.

How Binance.US calculates your fee tier

Binance.US calculates your fee tier based on your 30-day trading volume*, updated daily at 8:00 PM ET.

On the Tier 0 pair (currently BNB/USD only), Binance.US charges a flat 0% maker fee and 0.01% taker fee, no matter how much you trade.

Every other pair starts at 0% maker and 0.02% taker. Once your 30-day trading volume reaches $500 million, the taker fee on those pairs drops to 0.01%.

How to lower your Binance.US trading fees

You can reduce your trading fees on Binance.US by paying fees in BNB or by trading at scale.

  • Pay with BNB. If you pay your trading fees with BNB, you get a 5% discount on Advanced (Spot) Trading fees. Only BNB in your available balance qualifies. See paying with BNB.
  • Trade more. On pairs above Tier 0 (BNB/USD), your taker fee drops to 0.01% once your 30-day trading volume reaches $500 million.

On the Tier 0 pair, the maker and taker fees stay flat, independent of your trading volume, so the BNB discount is the main way to lower cost on that pair.

Deposit and withdrawal fees on Binance.US

Deposit and withdrawal fees are separate from trading fees and depend on which method you choose.

Deposits

  • Crypto: free.
  • Bank transfer (ACH): free.
  • Wire: $0, though your bank may charge its own fee.
  • Debit or credit card: 3.99%, though your card issuer may charge its own fee.

Withdrawals

  • Bank transfer (ACH): free.
  • Wire: a flat $25, plus any fee your own bank charges.
  • Crypto: withdrawal fees are shown prior to your transaction. Withdrawal fees may change from transaction to transaction. These fees are calculated using a dynamic fee model that considers several factors, including the total number of withdrawal, spot, and convert orders a user places, the estimated market costs of gas, BAM Trading's overall operational expenses and the total withdrawal amount of any one transaction.
  • Crypto (users in states powered by BitGo*, including New York and North Carolina): Withdrawals are charged a fee of 0.05% of the equivalent USD value of the amount withdrawn, calculated at the time of withdrawal. No gas fee is charged. In the event the calculated withdrawal fee is below a minimum threshold, which varies based on the asset and network, a nominal fee applies. The exact fee is shown before you confirm your withdrawal.
